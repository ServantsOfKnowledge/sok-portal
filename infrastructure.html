The infrastructure

Five open source parts, and the system that runs them.

Enterprise grade systems for the digital archiving ecosystem, in India and abroad.

Research Desk

The gapImported scanners were out of reach, and COVID broke the supply chain.

What we built

SOK's first three scanners followed an open design from the Internet Archive. When imports stalled, the team reverse-engineered it and began building improved scanners in India. In 2018 we had one scanner. Today we have 15.

See the digitisation workflow
  • Twin DSLR cameras on an overhead mount, 45° diffused LED panels
  • V-cradle for bound volumes; tethered capture to a laptop
  • 5,000 to 10,000 pages per scanner a day
  • No specialist operator, no imported parts

The gapDigital archiving is a chain of steps, and each book has to move through all of them.

What we are building

OpenScribe is a workflow management system for digital archiving. It captures the images at a SOK scanning station and pushes them to Repub and the steps after it. It is a crucial part of the work. It is in progress, and like all our hardware and software work it will be open source, released after testing.

  • One app per scanning station, working offline
  • Left and right pages shot together, with retakes
  • Each book pushed on to Repub and the steps after it
  • Open source, released after testing

The gapA raw scan is not yet a library, and the tools did not read Indian scripts.

What we built

When SOK began putting Indian gazettes online, the Internet Archive supported no script other than Latin. In 2018 Carl Malamud, Dr. Sushant Sinha and Omshivaprakash worked around it with Google's OCR, a first in India for Internet Archive collections. Repub is the open-source pipeline for this step: it picks up when a scanning session ends.

About Repub  ·  GitHub
  • Auto-crop, deskew and dewarp every page
  • Multi-language OCR with Tesseract
  • Searchable PDF with text layer, hOCR, thumbnails
  • Command line or web interface with job management

The gapA digital library needs five systems, and few libraries can run five.

What we built

A library needs a repository, search, a reader, OCR and proofreading, and preservation. Research Desk does all five in one install. Scans stay on the Internet Archive; Research Desk holds the catalogue and search index, so a laptop can host a portal for 88,000 books.

About Research Desk  ·  Visit it live
  • Full text of every page searchable, in Kannada, Hindi, Tamil and more
  • Page reader, reading lists, citations
  • OCR gives a draft; people proofread it
  • Free software (MIT), built on Frappe

The gapScans are only useful if people can get to them.

What we built

Public-domain works go straight onto the Internet Archive as the Jai Gyan: Bharat Ek Khoj collection. In-copyright works reach students within the law, and in Braille and DAISY for the print-disabled. Community centres do the scanning, and the SOK Academy trains the people who run them.

  • 1.3 million+ items in Jai Gyan: Bharat Ek Khoj
  • Bengaluru is our main centre; Mysuru mobilising
  • 200+ librarians and archivists trained
  • OAI-PMH, IIIF and other open standards

The gapMany centres, many people, and no single view of the work.

What we built

SOK Mantapa is the system that runs our digitisation centres. It records each scan and its quality check, and gives the team a live view of the day's work. It is SOK's own internal system.
