What it is

SOK Academy teaches people how digital archiving works and where the law stands, so that more collections can be digitised and shared with confidence.

It brings two things together that are rarely taught side by side: the legal and rights questions around digitising books, and the practical skills to do the digitising.

How it will run
  • Workshops and classes, taught in the open
  • Scheduled programs, each announced when it is ready
  • Hands-on programs to learn to digitise books
  • Open to librarians, researchers, educators, publishers, authors, students and the public
