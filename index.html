What We Do

A complete, open pipeline for digital archiving

From a bare shelf of decaying paper to a searchable, citable, world-readable record — every stage of the pipeline is open hardware, open software, and open access.

Open-hardware scanning

18+ book and document scanners, reverse-engineered from open designs and built in-house after import supply chains broke down during COVID — now replicated at every SoK centre.

Indian-language OCR

Text is captured in Unicode across 20+ Indian languages, producing fully searchable PDFs and hOCR — so a single quote can be traced back to its exact page.

Open access publishing

Public-domain works go straight onto the Internet Archive as the Jai Gyan: Bharat Ek Khoj collection — over 1.3 million items and counting, free for anyone, anywhere.

Accessibility first

Working with IDIA, digitised law libraries are turned into Braille and DAISY formats so visually-challenged students get the same access as anyone else.

Community digitisation centres

Anchored at Gandhi Bhavan, Bengaluru (capacity: 1.5 million pages/month) with new centres — starting with Mysuru — being mobilised across the country.

Servants of Knowledge Academy

Free training in scanner design, OCR, metadata, copyright and library management — 200+ librarians and archivists trained so far, with more sessions every year.

Why It Matters

Every year, a library is lost that no one archived in time.

Palm-leaf manuscripts crack. Newsprint yellows and crumbles. Regional-language books go out of print and vanish from private shelves. Institutional archiving is slow, expensive, and out of reach for most of the custodians of India's heritage — temple libraries, community trusts, small research institutes.

Our Approach

Open tools, run by communities, owned by no one.

SoK doesn't build a walled digital archive — it builds the open hardware, software and training so any library or institution can digitise its own collection and keep it free, forever. That's the difference between a top-down programme and a bottom-up public library that everyone builds together.
