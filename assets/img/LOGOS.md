# Logos kept in this repo

Every logo shown on the Collections and Partners pages is a local file here, so the pages do not depend on another site being up.

- `collections/*.jpg`: the collection's own image on the Internet Archive, from `https://archive.org/services/img/<collection-id>` (the id is the last part of the collection address, for example `bmshri`). Collections that only have the Internet Archive's grey placeholder (NLSIU-Open, Anaji, Kalanidhi, Rangamancha, House of Gandhi print) show initials instead.
- `partners/*.png`: from each institution's own website, saved at 256 px or smaller:
  - `apu.png` Azim Premji University (site icon), `rmrl.png` Roja Muthiah Research Library (site logo), `rbanms.png` RBANM's (site icon), `ninasam.png` Ninasam (site icon), `shankar.png` Shri Shankar College (site icon)
  - `nlsiu.png`, `sochara.png`, `konkani.png`, `idia.png`, `kanoon.png`, `ia.png`, `ult.png`, `madurai.png`, `navakarnataka.png`, `science.png`: saved earlier from the institutions' own sites or pages.

Not stored on purpose: press and video thumbnails on the Media and Events pages stay linked from the publishers, because those images belong to the outlets.
