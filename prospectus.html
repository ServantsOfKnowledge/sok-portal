How a gift works
  • Capture. Local operators digitise originals on site with open-hardware scanners, so fragile items stay put.
  • Process. Repub crops, deskews, dewarps, reads the text and builds metadata.
  • Publish. Public domain items go to Research Desk and the Internet Archive, crediting sponsors and custodians.
  • Report. Sponsors get item counts, page counts, collection links and media updates.

The challenge

Colleges, libraries and private collectors hold priceless Kannada, Sanskrit and other Indian-language works that the public cannot reach. Scanners are costly, software is fragmented and few teams are trained, so institutions choose between proprietary lock-in and loose toolkits.

In production

Repub

Crops, deskews, reads text and attaches metadata. Web app, API and client; over 100,000 books so far.

Open source

Research Desk

An open workspace for Indian-language books, manuscripts, photographs and recordings, for researchers and institutions.

Completes the suite

OpenScribe

The missing capture piece: connects DSLR and overhead cameras, converts images and sends metadata to Repub.

Why open hardware

Open scanner designs let institutions build and run their own stations without expensive proprietary systems.

Complete ownership

Open-source hardware and software, so no vendor lock-in.

Standard components

Standard DSLR cameras and easy-to-find parts, simple to maintain locally.

On-site protection

Fragile heritage items stay safely with their custodians while they are scanned.

Unified quality

Works with Repub for the same metadata and processing standards nationwide.

Covers software development, open hardware refinement and institutional deployment across our digitisation centres. We welcome partial funding, a single workstream, or another way of working that suits your organisation. All code, designs and documentation are released under open licences.

What the funding delivers
  • OpenScribe. Finish the capture station: multi-camera capture, insert camera, per-station login and hand-off to Repub.
  • Repub. Harden, document and extend the workflow for wider adoption.
  • Research Desk. Develop to a release institutions and researchers can use.
  • Open hardware. Refine the scanner design, publish build documents and build pilot units.
  • Training and rollout. Train partner institutions through the Servants Of Knowledge Academy so they can run stations themselves.
What lasts
  • A complete open workflow from capture to searchable digital book, for every Indian institution.
  • A national network of partner institutions able to preserve their collections independently.
  • Open-access collections on Research Desk and the Internet Archive, crediting custodians and sponsors.
  • Transparent reporting, with milestones on deployments, page counts and digitised works.
